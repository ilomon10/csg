Fix embedded GLB textures under the app CSP (spec 000 REQ-GEN-010, architecture 4.10): the GLB loader registers `imageElementTexturesPlugin`, so `GLTFParser` decodes textures through `<img>` from `blob:` (allowed by `img-src`) instead of `ImageBitmapLoader`'s `fetch` (blocked by `connect-src 'self'`); sRGB base color and `flipY = false` are unchanged. Fix preview directions (spec 003 REQ-PIX-005, AC-PIX-005.1): `setDirection(i)` now turns the +Z-facing model (REQ-AST-011) to face `i × 45°` counter-clockwise from screen-right, so `e` faces screen-right and `s` faces the camera; new exports `stageYaw` and `MODEL_FORWARD_YAW_OFFSET_RAD`.
