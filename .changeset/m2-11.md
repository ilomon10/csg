Pixel pipeline toon material (spec 003 REQ-PIX-011..014, 023 A8, 024, 038). New emitter-shaped toon stages `toonRamp` (`toon.ramp@1`), `toonRim` (`toon.rim@1`) and `toonCombine` in `pipeline/stages/toon.ts`, with CPU twins for tests. `pipeline/toon-material.ts` adds `createToonMaterial` (view-space toon ramp and rim from the binder's reserved uniforms, opaque, discards fragments with alpha below the shared `alpha.cutoff` uniform) and the scene MRT layout `createSceneMrt` (`output`, `normalDepth` = view normal + depth in output px from the pivot plane, `partId` from `Object3D.userData.partId`). `applyTintMaterial` takes optional `TintMaterialOptions` (binder, backend, mode, lighting) and then builds toon materials with the M1 tint and region-hide semantics; without options it still builds the unlit M1 materials. Part textures are now always sampled with mipmaps (`ensureMipmapped`).
