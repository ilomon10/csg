Easy and Pro workspaces (M3-15): Easy with a 55 % preview, category tabs from the Easy category data, tile groups, the body Shape group, one swatch row, Custom tiles with an "Edit in Pro" focus hand-off, and an actions bar (Undo, Redo, Randomize, Reset tab, Export).
