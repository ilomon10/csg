M2-14: pixel render pipeline and camera (spec 003 REQ-PIX-002, 003, 008, 010, 014, 023–025, 029, 034). `pipeline/camera.ts` places an orthographic camera for the `side` (0°), `three-quarter` (35°), `isometric` (30°, 2:1) and `custom` elevations with the union-bounds convention (screen y = y·cos e − z·sin e), the pixel-corner frustum of `computeFraming` and a camera-plane pivot snapped to the texel grid. `pipeline/render-pipeline.ts` adds `createPixelPipeline` / `PixelPipeline`: one HalfFloat MRT scene pass (`pass()` + `createSceneMrt`, attachments named by key, nearest, no MSAA, no mipmaps), the `DEFAULT_POST_STAGES` chain built from the emitter-shaped stages (`buildDefaultPostChain`), a three `RenderPipeline` with `outputColorTransform` off into an RGBA8 `NoColorSpace` cell target, tight top-left readback on both backends, post rebuilds only on structural settings changes, and `PIX_BACKEND_UNAVAILABLE` on WebGL2 without `EXT_color_buffer_float`. The pipeline drives the scene pass and advances three's node frame once per `render()`, so back-to-back export frames never reuse a stale scene pass or stale skinning bones. `pipeline/index.ts` re-exports the pipeline modules.
