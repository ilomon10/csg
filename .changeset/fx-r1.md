Renderer and pipeline robustness (M2 review FX-R1). `renderFrames` always releases the renderer lock: a failing restore after an export is reported through `onError` (`PIX_PALETTE_LUT_FAILED` or `PIX_PREVIEW_FAILED`) and the preview settings are re-applied at the next exclusive operation; a consumer that breaks out or calls `return()` releases it too. `PixelPipeline.setRenderSettings` is atomic: the palette LUT is built first, and on failure nothing changes; `setRenderSettings` on the renderer then returns `PIX_PALETTE_LUT_FAILED` (reported once through `onError`) and keeps the previous settings, also when a LUT worker rejects (REQ-GEN-016). A preview frame that throws stops the loop and pauses with `PIX_PREVIEW_FAILED` (REQ-PIX-039). New `paletteLutStats` diagnostics on the renderer. Node-frame `time`/`deltaTime` are set deterministically (0 in export, the drawn clip time in preview). `installFenceWait` returns `{installed, dispose}`, is reference-counted, closes its message channel on `PixelPipeline.dispose` and warns once when it cannot install. The rim sector tie rule (counter-clockwise) is implemented identically on GPU and CPU with an epsilon. Part textures are no longer mutated: tint materials sample a shared, reference-counted mipmapped clone (`mipmappedTexture`). The frame target's pose allocates nothing per frame and updates only the root joint's ancestor matrices. Legacy `toon.rim@1` helpers are marked `@deprecated`.
