M3 engine additions (M3-05). Compatibility rules (d) style and (e) species with reasons `style` and `species` (`checkCompatibility(part, body, character?)`, REQ-CMP-048). `EngineAssetRegistry.registerStyles`, `availableStyleCombos` and `styleDefinition` (REQ-CMP-043/045). The renderer renders the fallback (style, species) pair and reports `CMP_STYLE_UNSUPPORTED` through the new `onNotice` option and `notices`, never as an error; `prepareFrames` and `renderFrames` refuse an unavailable pair with `CMP_STYLE_UNSUPPORTED` (REQ-CMP-043/044). Parts listed in another part's `alsoOccupies`, and parts that do not fit the rendered pair, are not drawn (`assembly.hidden`, REQ-CMP-007/048). Per-part tint overrides (`PartSelection.tints`, REQ-CMP-015). Materials are owned per character assembly: the registry's cached part scenes are never re-materialed, so two renderers on one registry keep their own tints (`createPartMaterials`, `MaterialLinker`). New 3D view mode: `setViewMode('pixel' | '3d')`, `orbit`, `frameCharacter`, `orbitState`, an orbitable full-resolution perspective view (`createOrbitView`, REQ-UX-003). Grounding uses the character skeleton group's `soleOffsetM`: `groundOffsetY = −jointMinY(A) + soleOffsetM(G) × height × feet`, joint-only (REQ-ANA-008); characters on rigs with measured sole offsets render about 2 cm higher.
