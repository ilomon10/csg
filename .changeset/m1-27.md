Add the DOM-free, three-free retarget math in `@csg/engine/retarget` (REQ-ANM-023): `createRetargetPlan` precomputes per-bone `q_tRest · q_sRest⁻¹` and the leg-length ratio `k` (rest pelvis height above the lowest foot), and `retargetTracks` applies it to plain keyframe tracks with hemisphere-continuous rotations, delta-form pelvis and root translation scaled by `k`, scale pass-through, and a report of dropped tracks. Missing pelvis, root or foot bones fail with `AST_RIG_MISMATCH`.
