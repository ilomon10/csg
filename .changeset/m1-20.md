Add the bundled-asset loader and the asset registry (M1-20). `createGlbLoader` wraps `GLTFLoader` with the Meshopt decoder only (no Draco, no KTX2, no workers), allows only same-origin and `blob:` URLs, refuses GLBs declaring `KHR_draco_mesh_compression` or `KHR_texture_basisu`, converts body `_REGION` to a Float32 `regionId`, and caches by URL and SHA-256. `createAssetRegistry` registers part and clip manifests (parsed from JSON through `@csg/parts-schema`), lists parts by slot and clips by rig, resolves parts and clips with `CMP_PART_LOAD_FAILED` / `ANM_CLIP_LOAD_FAILED` reasons, and exposes `licenseOf` and `rigOf`. Also `checkCompatibility` (REQ-CMP-008), `restPoseOf` and `characterSkeletonGroupOf` (REQ-CMP-037).
