# Recipes

Each recipe is a short sequence of steps. Start with a recipe, then adjust the values until the sprite suits your game. Check every change in **Pixel** view at the size you will export.

> [!NOTE]
> **Planned.** The recipes are planned for a later release, together with the node reference they refer to.

## Before you start

- A built-in graph is never changed. Editing one first makes a copy in your project.
- Look at your sprite at its real size, not only at a zoomed-in view.
- Keep a copy of your project before you try a big change. Projects can be saved and loaded.

## The recipes

- [GameBoy look](./gameboy-look.md): four shades of green with a fine dither.
- [Thicker outline](./thicker-outline.md): a bolder silhouette that still reads at small sizes.
- [Custom palette](./custom-palette.md): limit every color to a palette you choose.
- [Cel-shaded bands](./cel-bands.md): flat, crisp shading in four bands.
