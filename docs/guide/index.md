# Welcome to Character Sprite Generator

Character Sprite Generator is a free, open-source tool that runs in your browser. You build a character from 3D parts, adjust its proportions, pick animations, and export a sprite sheet of pixel-art frames that a game engine can use.

## What you can make

- Side-view sprites for platformers, and top-down, three-quarter or isometric sprites for RPGs.
- Frames from 32 to 128 pixels, rendered in 1, 2, 4 or 8 directions.
- A PNG sprite sheet, a metadata file and a `CREDITS.txt` in one ZIP.

## Who it is for

- **Indie game developers** who need consistent character sprites without drawing every frame by hand.
- **Pixel artists** who want a clean base to paint over, or a look limited to a fixed palette.
- **Tech artists** who want to change the look with a node-based shader graph.

## What it is not

- It is not a 3D modelling tool. Sculpt, rig and texture your models in Blender, then upload them.
- It is not an AI image generator. Every frame is rendered from 3D parts.
- It is not a cloud service. Characters, projects and uploaded models stay in your browser, and there is no account.
