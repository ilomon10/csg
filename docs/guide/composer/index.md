# Composer

The composer is where you build a character. Pick a body, fill slots with parts such as hair, outfits and props, then recolor the result.

You can use the composer in two places:

- **Easy** shows the parts as big tiles, with color swatches. It is the quickest way to make a character.
- **Pro** shows the part library on the left and the **Parts** and **Colors** tabs in the inspector. It shows more detail.

Both change the same character, and both use the same undo history.

## Key terms

- **Body**: the base character. Every character has exactly one body.
- **Slot**: a named place on the body, such as `hair` or `torso`. A slot holds at most one part.
- **Part**: one item that fills a slot, such as a hat or a shirt.
- **Tint**: a color for one channel, such as skin or primary. A character has seven tints.
- **Style** and **species**: the overall look and kind of character, such as Chibi or Human. See [Styles and species](../anatomy/styles-and-species.md).

## Build a character

1. Choose a body and a style.
2. Click a slot, then click a part to equip it. A part can take more than one slot, so the editor clears the slots it needs.
3. Choose the colors of skin, hair and outfit.
4. Save the character as a file, or leave it in the project. See [Save, load and share](./save-load-share.md).
